# Design review — M4 tasks 05 `bot-combat`, 06 `bot-item-routing`, 04b `bot-tunables-and-ci-fts` (architect, 2026-10-30)

**Scope reviewed:** `m4-bots` at 8e41937: `Bots/VeloxBotCombat.*` (settings, fire rule, target choice, overlay, projectile threat, ballistic arrive, perception / overlay / act glue), `Bots/VeloxBotItemRouting.*` (scoring assembly, candidate selection, committed score, Duel min score / denial, refusal guard, the router state machine), `Bots/VeloxBotDecision.*` (fight score, `ChooseGoal`), `Bots/VeloxItemValue.*`, `Bots/VeloxBotController.*` (phases, `GetGoalTarget` Fight case, `EnsureMove`, off-mesh recovery, `Tick` budget), `Bots/VeloxBotPathFollowingComponent.*` (maneuver tick incl. the rocket-jump air braking, link entry), `Bots/VeloxBotSubsystem.cpp` (`RecordBotTick`, `VeloxBotBudget`), `Bots/VeloxBotSettings.h`, `Bots/VeloxBotTypes.h`, `Config/DefaultEngine.ini` (agent radius), `Config/DefaultGame.ini` (bot settings), `Tools/editor/create_item_test_map.py` (ramp, drop links), `Tools/editor/create_item_content.py` (`BotBaseValue` tiers), `infra/ci/fts.sh`, the test sections `Velox.Bots.Decision/ItemRouting/ShouldFire/CombatRules/Maneuver` and the `FtItemRouting`/`FtOffMesh` console FTs — against ADR 0005 §2, §4, §6–§10, §13, §14 with D4, D6, D10–D12 and the three reports (`notes/05-report.md`, `06-report.md`, `04b-report.md`). No build and no soak were run by the reviewer; numbers are the reports'. Q3 references are from memory of `ai_dmq3.c` (`BotAggression`, `BotWantsToRetreat`, `BotCheckAttack`) and marked so.

**Verdict: merge-ready after one must-fix (F1, the fight/item balance, dev-hard, ≈ 1–2 h) — everything else is a follow-up that must land before M4-07's soak is accepted, not before this merge.** The three designs are right where it matters: (1) combat is layered as world-free rules + thin glue that writes only `FVeloxBotInput`, bots fire at `Now` through the authority-local ability path with `rewind_ms = 0` (352/352 shots in the 05 run), and the maneuver's view requests sit on top of the aim model instead of inside it; (2) item routing is a per-frame state machine that honours D10 exactly (one `FindPathSync` per frame, one bot at a time, every query counted), scores from the live attributes through the ADR 0004 §9 API only, and keeps the pure `ChooseGoal` as the single decision point; (3) 04b moved the literals into data without changing behaviour and gave the FTs a CI entry point. What is wrong is a **tuning** of the fight score (the lead's main open item): with `FightBaseScore 20`, `FullStrength 100` and `WeaponStrength` never filled, a visible enemy beats every item short of a powerup, and D12 then parks the bot inside the fight leash — the pickup collapse (69–107 → 14–21 per 120 s) is the predictable result, not a bug in the routing. The ruling (D13) changes three data values, defines `WeaponStrength`, and adds a Fight↔Item hysteresis; the mechanism stays.

## 1. Rulings on the open items (recorded as ADR 0005 D13–D19)

| a | **Fight vs item balance** (04b §1): `FightBaseScore 20 × FightItemBias 1.5 = 30` beats items scoring 1.5–25; `WeaponStrength` is the struct default 1 | **D13 — values changed, `WeaponStrength` defined, hysteresis added.** `FightBaseScore 20 → 12`, `FightFullStrength 100 → 200` (full strength = 100 health **and** 100 armor, so a 100/0 bot sits at 0.5), `FightWeakWeaponScale 0.5 → 0.25`; `WeaponStrength` = best owned weapon with ≥ 1 shot of ammo: its preference `Weight` ÷ the largest `Weight` in the preset's table (MG-only ≈ 0.2, SG ≈ 0.6, RL/RG ≈ 1). Fight replaces a current Item goal (and Item a current Fight) only when it wins by `GoalSwitchHysteresis`. Worked thresholds at Medium (bias 1.5, items 3 s away): 100/100 RL → threshold 18: Mega (12.5) loses, powerup (27.5) and an unowned weapon at 2 s (20) win — a healthy, armed bot stands and fights; 100/0 RL → 9: Mega, YA (17.5), RA (22.5) win, shards and ammo (≈ 1.3) lose — the bot routes to armor while shooting; 60/0 MG-only → 2.2: even a 25-health (2.5) wins — the Q3 retreat; 100/100 MG-only → 7.5: weapons (15) win — the Q3 "aggression 0 without a real gun". | c | **06 refinements**: `DuelMinItemScore 3`, `DuelMajorItemMinNeed 0.8`, `CommittedScore`, refusal guard 1.5 s / 10 s, airborne start projection | **D16 — all confirmed, with units and a soak expectation.** `DuelMinItemScore 3` is "one shard one second away" in value-per-second units: below it a dueller camps the next major instead of hoovering shards (without it the camping branch could never trigger on a map full of minor items). `DuelMajorItemMinNeed 0.8` is denial: a usable major is worth ≥ 0.8 of its tier while it is up, so a full dueller takes RA at need 0.05 (72/4 = 18 at 3 s beats its own fight score 14.4 at Hard) — what a human dueller does; `Need 0` (the pickup would refuse) stays 0. `CommittedScore` (gate widened by the travel time at commit, capped `MaxCommitTravelSeconds 8`) removes the early-mover oscillation; keep. | d1 | **`AgentRadius 45` + off-mesh recovery (ring search)** instead of a follower corner offset (04b) | **D17 — accepted, with a mapping convention and a terminal escape.** Recast erodes by `ceil(45/19) = 3` cells = 57 cm per side (was 2 cells = 38 cm), so string-pulled paths clear the inner corners of the cross by ≈ 20 cm more than the capsule needs — the correct fix at the source; a corner offset in the follower would fight the engine's own corridor and re-create the problem at every acute corner. ## 2. Code checked and found correct (recorded so the next pass does not re-derive it)

- **Combat glue writes only `Pending`** (`VeloxBotCombat.cpp:925–1206`): `Overlay` touches `WishDirWorld/WishScale/bJumpHeld`, `Act` touches `View/SelectSlot/bFireHeld`; nothing reaches the pawn or the CMC; the stream is the controller's (`Stream.FRand` in `HandleDamaged`, `RollChance`, flip timing, aim noise); no `FMath::Rand*` in `Bots/`.
- **Phase order holds** (`VeloxBotController.cpp:1395–1451`): Perceive → Decide + `EnsureMove` → Navigate → `UpdateCombat` → `Act` → `ApplyInput`, so the overlay sees the follower's wish and the maneuver's view requests override the aim model's output in `Act` (`:1181–1201`), never the other way round; `Combat.Aim.Aim = Pending.View` after an override keeps the OU state continuous (no snap back after a rocket jump).
- **Perception** (`:781–923`): candidates = `PlayerArray` pawns that the lag-comp subsystem has registered (living, hittable), minus self; FOV from the bot's actual view; centre trace then head trace on `Velox_TraceChannel_Weapon` with the target ignored (world-only LOS); memory removal by `max(LastSeen, Heard)`; sticky target via `SelectTarget` (unit-tested); stagger `BotIndex × 1/60 mod Interval`. Two traces per candidate only when the first is blocked (cheaper than the ADR's "two per candidate").
- **Fire rule** (`:422–468`): range band, splash safety (`Range ≤ R + 50` or wall hit within `R + 50` → `SelfSplash`), cone or ground-rocket test, direct-hit radius for non-splash; `bWeaponReady` requires no pending switch and `SwitchEndTime` passed; the maneuver suppresses the rule while it wants the launcher so the jump's rocket is not spent in the run-up (`:1159–1180`).
- **Rocket-jump air braking** (`VeloxBotRocketJump::ArriveWish`, `VeloxBotPathFollowingComponent.cpp:609–619`): descending-root ballistic time, needed XY velocity, wish = error direction with a 30 cm/s deadband — vanilla air accelerate along a wish opposed to the velocity brakes, which is why a 4 m platform is hit from a 1500 cm/s launch (FT: rockets taken 4.4–4.5 s, apex 660 cm, 14 health + 28 armor of self-damage — within the §4 estimate).
- **Router D10 compliance** (`VeloxBotItemRouting.cpp:799–925`, `1050–1116`): `BeginEvaluation` runs no query; `StepEvaluation` runs exactly one `FindPathSync` and sets `LastStepFrame`; `UpdateBot` refuses a second step in the same frame and a second active bot; every query calls `Bot.NotePathQuery()` so `VeloxBotBudget paths=/s` counts router and controller queries alike (`EnsureMove :1038`, recovery `:1188`). Partial paths mark `Unreachable` for 5 s with the current filter and are cleared on a filter change; a failed projection does not (correct: it proves nothing).
- **Scoring inputs** come from `UVeloxHealthSet`, the ammo attributes through `UVeloxCombatSettings::AmmoTypes`, `OwnsIndex`, the definition and `IsAvailable/GetTimeUntilRespawn` — the ADR 0004 §9 API only; `Items/` is untouched.
- **Decision wiring**: `FillEnemyInput` counts a remembered-but-unseen Fight enemy as present so a cadence evaluation does not drop a D12 chase; the enemy's death or memory expiry sets `bEventPending` (`:859–862`) and the next evaluation returns `Roam` for a Fight whose pawn is gone (`:1211–1214`). A newly visible target forces an immediate Fight-vs-Item decision (`:845–858`), which is the right moment for D13's comparison.
- **Off-mesh recovery** (`VeloxBotController.cpp:1066–1197`): checked every 0.5 s on the ground with the default query extent (what `MoveTo` uses, so "off" means "no path can start here"); 1 s grace; rings 150/300/600/1000 × 12 directions, candidates no higher than step height, thin static sweep at step height (slides along walls), nearest-point fallback, previous target avoided; a direct two-point `MoveTo` through the same follower (no teleport, no velocity write); `OnMoveCompleted` re-checks at once. 04b's runs: 0/1/0/1 excursions, all recovered.
- **Tunables**: every value named in the three reports is a `Config` property or a preset field (`UVeloxBotSettings`, `UVeloxBotCombatSettings`, `UVeloxBotItemRouter`, `AVeloxBotController`, `UVeloxBotPathFollowingComponent`); `DefaultGame.ini` carries the settings explicitly; the pure cores keep plain struct defaults as 04b's acceptance required.

## 3. Defects, ranked (file:line → fix, verdict)

1. **F1 — must-fix before merge (dev-hard, task 04c-a, ≈ 1–2 h): fight score dominates; `WeaponStrength` never filled; no Fight↔Item hysteresis.** `Config/DefaultGame.ini:103–105` (20 / 100 / 0.5) → 12 / 200 / 0.25 (D13). `VeloxBotItemRouting.cpp:1144–1156` fills `In.Health/Armor` and the fight params but not `In.WeaponStrength` (`VeloxBotDecision.h:66` default 1): add `VeloxBotItemRouting::WeaponStrength(const FVeloxRoutingBotFacts&, TConstArrayView<FVeloxBotWeaponPreference>)` (pure: best owned weapon with ≥ 1 shot → `Weight / max Weight`; `GatherBotFacts` gains the weapon → ammo-tag map it needs) and call it in `FinishEvaluation`. `VeloxBotDecision.cpp:197–209`: with `In.Current.Type == Item` the fight must exceed `Item->Score × GoalSwitchHysteresis` to win; with `Current.Type == Fight` the item must exceed `Bias × Fight × GoalSwitchHysteresis`. Unit rows in `Velox.Bots.Decision` (the four worked cases of §1a, plus the two hysteresis directions) and one `Velox.Bots.ItemRouting` row for `WeaponStrength` (MG-only 20/90, RL 1.0, empty RL → MG). Regression evidence: the FFA run of 04b (3 Medium, 120 s × 3) repeated with the pickup band of §4.
2. **F2 — follow-up before 07 acceptance (dev-hard, task 04c-b): ramp-slab oscillation has no stuck strike** (`VeloxBotPathFollowingComponent.cpp:465–496` hop decision; `VeloxBotController.cpp:1293–1311` speed-only stuck rule). Fix per D18: headroom sweep gating `bJumpHeld`, progress-based stuck rule (`StuckProgressCm` 100 on `AVeloxBotController`), unit rows for both pure parts (`VeloxBotRules::UpdateStuckProgress`, the sweep height from `JumpVelocity`/`GravityZ`), and the 04b ramp run repeated (`VeloxBotStuck ≤ 2`, no bot within 300 cm of the ramp underside for > 5 s).
3. **F3 — follow-up before 07 acceptance (dev, task 04c-c): hearing and dodge are unobservable** (`VeloxBotCombat.cpp:725–731` arms silently; `:938–943` starts the dodge silently). Fix per D15: the two log lines, the two counters in `Describe`, `velox.bots.FtCombatEvents`, `fts.sh` 6/6. The FT needs a damage call with an instigator (the `velox.bots.Kill` path has it, `VeloxBotCheats.cpp:410–430`) and a projectile spawn from a definition (the bot subsystem registers it in `BeginPlay` on authority).
4. **F4 — follow-up before 07 acceptance (dev, task 04c-c): `max_us` excludes every fire-held tick** (`VeloxBotController.cpp:1442` passes `State.Pending.bFireHeld`; `VeloxBotSubsystem.cpp:536–540`). Fix per D14: `bFireTick` = the fire ability's `NextFireTime` advanced during this tick's `ProcessAbilityInput`. `max_us` then means what §13 says.
5. **F5 — follow-up (dev, task 04c-c): `FtItemRouting` is not peaceful** (`VeloxBotFunctionalTest.cpp:790–998` never sets `velox.bots.Peaceful`, unlike the other three FTs at `:367`, `:733`, `:1156`). The Hard bot sees the `-game` local player, every visibility change re-triggers an evaluation and the 04b report's 542-frame / 40-query run is the result. Set `Peaceful 1` in the FT like the others; add "frames ≤ 3 × `PathQueryCandidates` per evaluation" to its checks so the regression is caught.
6. **F6 — follow-up (dev, task 04c-c): off-mesh recovery can loop forever** (`VeloxBotController.cpp:1104–1197`: attempts are unbounded; a bot stranded *below* mesh level never finds a candidate ≤ `Feet.Z + StepHeight`, walks the "nearest" fallback into a wall, times out after 4 s and repeats). Fix per D17: `OffMeshMaxAttempts` (6) → `result=giveup` → suicide through the environment-damage path → B28 respawn. Log line parsed by 07 (`giveup` = 0 expected).
7. **Minor (no task) — `FVeloxBotDecisionInput::bEnemyVisible` is set for a remembered, unseen chase enemy** (`VeloxBotItemRouting.cpp:441–458`). Behaviour is intended (D12); the name is not. Rename to `bEnemyPresent` when the file is next touched; the comment already says what it means.
8. **Minor (no task) — router saturation at 16 bots.** One step per server frame and `PathQueryCandidates 4` give 60/4 = 15 evaluations per second for the whole server; 16 bots at `GoalReevaluateSeconds 1.0` (Expert 0.75) exceed it and the cadence stretches gracefully (`VeloxBotRouteStats max_deferred_frames`). Accepted for M4 (≤ 8 bots in every planned soak). The knob, if 07 shows `max_deferred_frames` > 30 with 8 bots, is `PathQueryCandidates 4 → 3`, not the frame rule.
9. **Minor (no task) — a second attacker is ignored while a target is visible** (`HandleDamaged :720–723`). Target stickiness covers the common case; Q3's switch-to-attacker is deferred with the rest of multi-enemy logic.
10. **Minor (no task) — ammo `BotBaseValue 10` is low next to Q3's fuzzy ramp** (which rises toward the weapon's own weight as the clip empties). With D13 a bot holding an empty RL and nothing else scores `WeaponStrength ≈ 0.2` (MG) and may strafe rather than walk 3 s for rockets (score ≈ 2.5). Watch `fire=noammo` in 07's debug lines; if it is frequent, the fix is in the item assets (ammo tier 10 → 25 when the owned weapon is empty would be a `Need` change, architect first).

Accepted residuals (not defects): the Overlay replaces the wish for one tick when a Roam goal meets a newly visible enemy (the next tick's evaluation makes it Fight or Item); `GetNavFilter()` still walks the link list per call (previous review, minor 6); a camping dueller leaves the camp spot when the enemy appears (it fights or routes by D13, re-camps after memory expiry) — the 06 Duel runs still took Mega within 0.006–2.5 s of respawn in most cycles; the `-game` FT worlds carry a local player pawn that peaceful bots ignore but perception still traces (cost only).
