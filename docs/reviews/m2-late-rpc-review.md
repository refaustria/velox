# Review — M2 late reliable RPCs: RocketJumpRun self-splash under loss and remote-shooter switch timing (architect, 2026-10-29)

**Scope reviewed:** `docs/tasks/m2-combat/notes/rj-late-fire-investigation.md` and the round-4 logs (`Saved/CI/evidence-rj-late-fire/nettest/round-4/`), task `08e-switch-timing-remote-shooter.md` (on `m2-combat`), reports 05/05b/08c/08d; code on `m3-gameloop` (identical to `m2-combat` for these files): `Weapons/VeloxGameplayAbility_FireProjectile.*`, `VeloxGameplayAbility_Fire.*`, `VeloxGameplayAbility_FireHitscan.cpp`, `VeloxGameplayAbility_WeaponSwitch.cpp`, `VeloxWeaponInventoryComponent.cpp`, `VeloxProjectile.*`, `VeloxShotValidation.*`, `VeloxFireTiming.h`, `VeloxShotTargetData.h`, `Movement/VeloxMovementComponent.*`, `Movement/VeloxNetworkMoveData.*`, `Net/VeloxLagCompensationSubsystem.*`, `Combat/VeloxCombatSettings.h`, `infra/ci/nettest.sh`; engine `CharacterMovementComponent.cpp` (UE 5.8: `ReplicateMoveToServer` l.8907–9090, `ServerMove_HandleMoveData` l.9928, `ServerMove_PerformMovement` l.9967–10060), `NetDriver.h` packet simulation (l.471–558). ADR 0003 B15, B18, B19, B25, B26, B30–B34; ADR 0002 A29, A32, A35.

**Verdict: both issues are one defect class — a decision that belongs on the client's move timeline is taken from the arrival time of a reliable RPC, which lags the (unreliable, packetdup-repaired) move stream by up to RTT + 80 ms after one lost packet.** Ruling: (1) the projectile shot's self-splash data travels in the move payload of the fire move (A2, reduced to what B25 needs; A1 rejected); (2) every shot's origin is validated against the shooter's eye at `FireMoveTimeStamp` (B, for hitscan and projectiles); (3) the server's remote-shooter switch timeline loses the receive-time carry, its tolerance becomes the one resend tolerance (0.5 s), and the inventory owns the server-side drop completion. Three `dev-hard` tasks: `08f` (move-carried shot, B35/A36), `08g` (eye-at-stamp origin, B36), `08h` (switch timeline, B37 — absorbs `08e`). `MaxRewindSeconds` stays 0.30 pending the human (question 19), with the jitter numbers added.

## 1. Evidence (round 4, `RocketJumpRun --repeat 10`, 100 ms one-way / 2 %, 9/10)

Client: `VeloxRocketJump: fire+jump t=2.380`, `VeloxProjectileFired seq=1 fire_ts=1.750139 flight_ms=55.6 event_ts=1.805739`, `VeloxSelfSplash role=client ts=1.808481 dist=36.08 points=87`. Server: `VeloxMoveRepaired ts=1.750139 dt=0.008334 slot=extra` and `ts=1.758472 slot=old` at 20.32.02:551 — the dual RPC `{F−1, F}` of the fire frame was lost and repaired by the next RPC 16 ms later (A32/A35 work as designed); but the fire target data, a reliable RPC in the same lost packet, arrived at 20.32.02:788, **237 ms later** (RTT + resend): `VeloxShotOriginSnap seq=1 err_cm=108.9 reason=distance` (the pawn was 109 cm higher by then), `VeloxProjectileShot client_ts=1.750139 last_ts=2.016827 flight_ms=104.0 event_ts=1.854157` (flight recomputed from the snapped origin), `VeloxSelfSplash role=server ts=2.025161 dist=110.15 points=63` (overdue event at the next move). Corrections: 9.01 cm at `ts=1.816815` (`svel=541.6`: the server had only the jump, no impulse), 6.56 cm at `ts=2.033495` (`kseq=1`: the late impulse). Apex 365.6 vs 713.2 cm. No clamp (0.267 < 0.30), no deadline, no reset — every mechanism behaved as written; the premise was wrong.

The 08c `--lag 125` rail rejection has the same shape: `wait=0.090` client vs `wait=0.373` server — the server's `NextFireTime` had been carried forward by bunched resent MG shots (`VeloxFireTiming::AdvanceNextFireTime` on receive time: `Next + Interval` whenever `Now − Next ≤ FrameDt`, six bunched arrivals = +0.6 s), so its switch ended 0.28 s after the client's and the rail at the client's switch end met the B18 gate with 0.30 s remaining > 0.25.

## 2. Rulings

| # | Question | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Root cause of the RocketJumpRun failure | **Confirmed as stated in the investigation, defect (a).** B25 silently assumes the fire RPC is processed no later than the move stream reaches `EventTime`. Under loss the move stream is repaired within one RPC (16 ms) while the reliable RPC waits for the NAK-driven resend (≈ RTT + 80 ms, measured in 08). For a floor rocket the window is the flight time (≈ 55 ms); any loss of the fire packet at RTT > 55 ms misses it. Probability ≈ loss rate per rocket (2 % here), cost a 9 cm + 6.5 cm correction and half the jump — a movement-feel defect (priority 1). |
| 2 | A1: hold the owner's moves until the shot arrives | **Rejected.** (i) Lag-switch surface: a client that tags moves and withholds the RPC freezes its server pawn for the timeout on every shot; other clients extrapolate it (B30) and then see a teleport when the held moves run in one frame; the hitbox history gets a flat segment. (ii) Holding needs its own dedup (duplicates of held moves pass `VerifyClientTimeStamp` again) and starves acks for one RTT. (iii) It still needs a per-move tag in the payload — the same serialisation change as A2 with none of A2's benefits. |
| 3 | A2: shot data in the move payload | **Adopted, reduced to what B25 needs (B35, ADR 0002 A36).** The fire move of a *projectile* weapon carries `FVeloxMoveShot {ShotSeq, WeaponIndex, FireMoveTimeStamp, Origin (NetQuantize100), Dir (NetQuantizeNormal)}` ≈ 19 B, one bit otherwise. The server registers the owner's self-splash from the move stream, *before* the fire move is simulated; the GAS RPC keeps everything else (projectile spawn, ammo, cue, switch finalisation, `ClientConfirmHit`). The fire move is an event move (A29) and wins old-move slot 1 (A32), its predecessor slot 2 (A35): a lost fire packet is repaired in the original partition by the very next RPC — exactly what round 4 shows the move stream doing on its own. This is Quake's model (the attack is in the usercmd, duplicated by `cl_packetdup`); Lyra has nothing here (reliable target data only, no self-knockback prediction). A separate unreliable duplicated RPC was considered and rejected: no ordering guarantee against the move stream (a point-blank plasma's event can be due inside the fire move itself) and a second resend mechanism where the old-move slots already exist. |
| 4 | What the move-shot does *not* do | The projectile is still spawned by the RPC, forward-simulated by `ping/2` as today (Q3 does not back-date missiles either; a late rocket appears ≤ 1 RTT late to other players on ≈ 2 % of shots under loss — unchanged, cosmetic). At move time the server consumes the weapon's fire-rate token (the fire ability's `ServerBucket`; the RPC then skips the bucket for that sequence) and checks ammo is affordable; ammo is paid by the RPC. Residual cheat surface: a modified client that withholds the RPC gets self-knockback at the honest rate without paying ammo while it owns ≥ 1 rocket — worthless as a weapon, a trivial mobility gain, logged as `VeloxMoveShotOrphan` (an anti-cheat signal for M6, like B32's deferred per-shooter tolerance). |
| 5 | B: validate the origin against the eye at `FireMoveTimeStamp` | **Adopted for hitscan and projectiles (B36).** The server keeps a per-move ring `(ClientTimeStamp, eye after the move)` on `UVeloxMovementComponent` (128 entries ≈ 1.07 s at 120 Hz, 2 KB per remote pawn) pushed in `MoveAutonomous`; `FireMoveTimeStamp` moves into the base target data (hitscan gains it, +4 B per shot, LG +80 B/s). `ValidateOrigin` compares against the ring eye at the stamp (exact match expected: the stamp is the previous move's end, which the ring holds verbatim; newer than the newest entry → the live eye, which *is* that eye when the RPC overtakes the move in the same packet). Lag-comp history is not reused: it is sampled per server frame, not per move, and holds capsule poses, not eyes. `MaxOriginErrorCm 75` is unchanged (it now absorbs only prediction error; tightening is deferred to evidence: the max `err_cm` over the soaks). The hitscan *rewind* under a late RPC is still clamped to `MaxRewindSeconds` — an honest late rail may miss a moving target; the fix for that is hitscan in the move stream, deferred (see §5). `late_ms` goes into the `VeloxShot`/`VeloxProjectileShot` lines so the forced-loss runs can show it. |
| 6 | 08e item 1: honest rail rejected at 125 ms one-way | **Two defects, both fixed in B37.** (i) The server runs B19's one-frame carry on *receive* time: bunched resent shots push `NextFireTime` ahead by one interval each, and `RefireWait` turns that into a longer server-side switch. The carry is a local-rhythm rule; for a remote shooter the server keeps `LastAcceptedReceiveTime` per weapon and the refire wait is `max(0, LastAccepted + Interval − Now)`. (ii) `SwitchTimingToleranceSeconds 0.25` is below the measured resend compression (RTT + 80 ms = 0.33 s at the ping limit); reliable in-order delivery bounds the skew between the switch RPC and the fire RPC by that compression, so the switch gate needs the same tolerance as the fire-rate bucket: `FireRateJitterToleranceSeconds` (0.5 s), one setting; `SwitchTimingToleranceSeconds` is deleted (setting, cvar, bootstrap field, `docs/content-bootstrap.md`). Cheat surface: a modified client gains ≤ 0.5 s per switch (Drop + Raise = 0.45 s, i.e. it can skip its switch animation) — indistinguishable on the wire from an honest client at the ping limit after one lost packet, the same argument as B32. The move-time switch gate (switch target data carrying a move stamp, compared with `FireMoveTimeStamp`, bounded by the B25 clamp window instead of the resend tolerance) is the better anti-cheat answer and is deferred to M6 with B32's item. |
| 7 | 08e item 2: B33 reject branch leaves the superseded switch without a drop end | **Fix the ownership, not the branch (B37).** The engine's retrigger ends the superseded instance *before* the new `ActivateAbility` runs, so no branch inside it can keep the old `WaitDelay`. On the server the inventory owns the switch timeline: `SetPendingIndex` arms a world-timer for the drop end (`CompletePendingDrop`: `SetCurrentIndex(Pending)` if still pending), cleared by `SetCurrentIndex`, `CompletePendingSwitch*`, `ResetToLoadout`; the switch ability's `OnDropFinished` on authority becomes a no-op. A rejected supersede then simply leaves the timer running. With ruling 6's tolerance the honest case that could hit the reject branch (switch RPC delayed by compression, second press on time → remaining ≈ 0.58 > Raise + 0.25) is also gone (limit 0.75). |
| 8 | 08e item 3: `MaxRewindSeconds` 0.30 vs ±20 ms netsim jitter | **No code change; the human decides question 19 with these numbers.** Needed rewind (B30/B34) = RTT + 20–40 ms; at 250 ms RTT with ±20 ms jitter per direction it reaches 0.33 s, so 0.30 clamps a few shots (08c run 1: 3 fallbacks, all still hits) and 0.35 would cover the jittered limit. Both are honest degradations (a 30 ms clamp is 18 cm at 600 cm/s). The acceptance rows at `--lag 125` are written for 0.30: hits ≥ 9/10, 0 rejections, 0 snaps; `hint=stamp ≥ n−1` is reported, asserted only if 0.35 is chosen. |
| 9 | 08e item 4: stale "bucket + spacing" comments | Folded into `08h` as the documentation item (list in the task). |
| 10 | The common rule | **B35 states it:** *facts that must be placed on the client's move timeline travel in the move stream; the server never derives rhythm from receive times; every receive-time decision uses the one resend tolerance.* Both issues fall out of it; it also names what stays reliable-RPC-timed on purpose (activation, ammo, cues, hit confirms) because those tolerate ≤ 1 RTT + resend. |

## 4. Interactions checked

- **A35 slots.** The fire move `F` is an event move; while unacked the slots are `{F, F−1}`; ≈ 55 ms later the splash move `S` (predicted knockback) becomes the newest event move and takes slot 1 with `S−1` in slot 2 — `F` is then repaired only if it was still lost after ≥ 3 consecutive RPC losses (2 %³), in which case it merges and the RPC path takes over with `VeloxShotLate` logged. Round 4 (one lost dual RPC) is repaired by the first following RPC.
- **Self-knockback channel (§3, B25).** Unchanged. `ServerBeginSelfKnockbackShot(Seq)` now runs at move time (earlier than today, same order as the client's own schedule); `ConfirmedSelfKnockbackSeq` may confirm a sequence before its projectile exists — harmless, the client's replay rule only compares sequences. Out-of-order between the move stream (seq 5) and a hitscan RPC (seq 4): `NewerShotSeq` is wrap-aware and monotone, no hole.
- **Explosion matching (`VeloxProjectile.cpp:432–463`).** A late RPC spawns the projectile after the event already ran: `!HasMoveTimedEvent` → `bSkipOwner` ("already resolved in the owner's move stream") — the existing branch. The deadline (`FwdSim + MaxRewind`) is unchanged.
- **Switch RPC lost, fire move on time.** The move-shot may name a weapon the server does not yet see as current/pending (its switch RPC is still in flight; reliable ordering keeps the *fire RPC* behind it). The move-shot therefore accepts any **owned** projectile weapon; the current/pending check stays on the RPC (`CanActivateAbility`, B18). Hence `WeaponIndex` in the record.
- **Death / new life.** `CancelAllMoveTimedEvents` (B25/B27) and `ResetToLoadout` clear the inventory's move-shot records with the shot sequence.
- **Bandwidth.** 19 B per projectile fire move plus its resends in slot 1/2 while unacked (12–24 RPCs at 60–120 Hz, ≤ 1 RTT): ≤ 0.5 KB/s for RL, up to ≈ 3.6 KB/s while holding plasma fire at 250 ms RTT. Accepted for M2; the 9 B variant (direction only, origin = server eye at the move start) is the mitigation if a soak shows it matters.
- **Forced loss in tests.** Game code cannot drop a packet; `net.PktLossMinSize` (NetDriver.h l.489) could target the larger fire packets but is brittle. Deterministic substitute: non-shipping server cvar `velox.combat.DebugHoldShotRpc <ms> <slot> [count]` holds the next `count` target-data RPCs of that weapon for `ms` before `OnServerTargetData` runs (the ability instance waits, as it does for a real resend). Faithful for RL (RocketJumpRun) and RG (FireRun) whose intervals exceed the hold; not for MG/LG (an activation of shot 2 would overlap the held instance, which a real in-order resend never does) — hence the slot filter.

## 5. Deferred (recorded in B35)

- Hitscan in the move stream (the full Quake model): removes the rewind clamp on late rails. Trigger: the forced-hold FireRun shows held rails missing a target they would otherwise hit, or the playtest reports "my rail did not register" at the ping limit.
- Move-time switch gate (switch target data with a move stamp; tolerance = the B25 clamp window): M6 anti-cheat pass, together with B32's per-shooter tolerance.
- `FVeloxMoveShot` without origin (9 B) and a tighter `MaxOriginErrorCm`: on bandwidth or snap-log evidence.
- `MaxRewindSeconds` 0.35: human, question 19.
