| 3 | Hold `late_ms ∈ [280, 360]` (observed 275.0 and 466.7) | **Rule corrected (B39): assert what the hold adds, not the absolute.** `late_ms` = processed client-move time minus the fire stamp at release. ## 4. Recommendation text for STATUS question 19 (for the lead to put to the human)

> **19. `MaxRewindSeconds` — architect recommends 0.35.** 0.30 does not keep B34's promise at the 250 ms ping limit: with the ±20 ms jitter the needed rewind is 0.33 s, and 08h's five `--lag 125` runs show the clamp on 2–6 of 11 rails (`hint=stamp` 5–9/11, median rewind 280–300 = the clamp). 0.35 covers it. Cost: only shooters at 200–250 ms ping hit up to 50 ms further into the past, i.e. the victim can be hit ≤ 0.35 s after leaving the line of fire (18 cm per 30 ms at full speed); Valve ships 1.0 s, Q3 unlagged is unbounded, so 0.35 is still conservative. Separate and not fixed by either value: a rail whose packet is lost at the ping limit arrives one resend later and would need 0.6 s — one rail in ~50 for such a player at 2 % loss, none at ≤ 120 ms ping. The fix for that (hitscan carried in the move stream, like the rocket shot since 08f) is deferred to M6 unless the playtest reports rails not registering at high ping. Options: 0.30 (current) / **0.35 (recommended)**.
