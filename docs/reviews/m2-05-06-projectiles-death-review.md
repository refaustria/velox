# Design review — M2 tasks 05 `projectiles-selfknockback` and 06 `death-respawn-gamemode` (architect, 2026-10-29)

**Scope reviewed:** task 05 (feae626): `Weapons/VeloxProjectile.*`, `Weapons/VeloxProjectileSim.h`, `Weapons/VeloxGameplayAbility_FireProjectile.*`, `Movement/VeloxMovementComponent.*` and `Movement/VeloxNetworkMoveData.*` (diff `06df0fa..feae626`), `Weapons/VeloxCombatFeedbackComponent.cpp` (impact-cue hook), `Tests/VeloxProjectileTest.cpp`, `Tests/VeloxCombatTests.cpp` (`KnockbackSeq`), `Tests/VeloxSavedMoveTest.cpp`; task 06 (be0184d, merged 3356731): `Character/VeloxCharacter.*` (death flow, corpse, death camera, colour), `GameModes/VeloxGameMode.*`, `GameModes/VeloxSpawnSelection.h`, `GameModes/VeloxGameState.h`, `GameModes/VeloxPlayerController.*`, `GameModes/VeloxPlayerState.*`, `UI/VeloxHUD.cpp`, `Tests/VeloxDeathTests.cpp` — against ADR 0003 §3, §5, §7, §8, §9, §10 with B1–B24 and ADR 0002 A17, A18, A19, A24, A28, A29, A32, A33. The 04b base (`ValidateServerShot`, `FinalisePendingSwitch`, B18/B19) is the reference for the projectile ability. Engine claims verified in `/opt/UnrealEngine-5.8` (`CharacterMovementComponent.cpp`, line numbers below). The failed soak `Saved/CI/20261025-034018-2760259/nettest/KnockbackRun-*` was read for §3.

**Verdict: accept both designs; two Must-fix items (one per task) go to 05b (dev-hard, ~2–3 h) and 06b (dev, ~1.5 h), both before 08. Nothing blocks 04c; 08 starts after 05b and 06b.** The move-timed owner self-splash is a genuine improvement over the ADR's "server explosion → one correction": it makes the rocket jump a discrete event at a move boundary in A33's sense (same move on both ends, event move important, repaired by A32's OldMove choice), and the two online runs with `corrections=0` are the expected result. The recorded channel is implemented as §3 specifies, the response snapshot (A18) and the live-prediction save/restore around the replay are right. What must be fixed is a sequence-number hole (the per-life `ShotSeq` counter and `ConfirmedSelfKnockbackSeq` can drift more than 127 apart, which silently drops a predicted rocket jump from a replay) and, for 06, that the test-map dummies die after two rails and respawn as plain pawns at random starts, which breaks 08's `FireRun` before it starts. The death flow's next-tick teardown is accepted as a B8 amendment. ADR 0003 gained **B25–B29**. The KnockbackRun watch item is, on the evidence in the logs, not a container defect (§3).

## 2. Defects, ranked (file:line → fix)

1. **Must-fix (05b) — predicted self-knockback silently dropped from a replay when `ShotSeq` runs > 127 ahead of `ConfirmedSelfKnockbackSeq`.** `Movement/VeloxMovementComponent.h:107` (`ShouldReplayPredictedKnockback`, `int8(Seq − Confirmed) > 0`), `Movement/VeloxMovementComponent.cpp:752` (client takes the response value unconditionally), `Weapons/VeloxWeaponInventoryComponent.h:62` (`AllocateShotSeq` counts every weapon's shots for the life of the PlayerState). The client's `Confirmed` is the server's value at the last correction's check time; the server only moves it on projectile shots (`ServerBeginSelfKnockbackShot` → `Seq − 1`). Two real cases: (a) a new life: the CMC starts at 0 while the counter may be at 130+; (b) 127+ hitscan shots since the last RL/PG shot (LG at 20 Hz: 6.4 s). In both, a correction whose snapshot predates the fire RPC (anything in flight during the RTT/2 before the shot — strays, echoes of an earlier impulse) replays with `int8(Seq − Confirmed) ≤ 0` → `PredictedKnockbackForReplay` returns nothing → the replay loses the rocket jump, the client snaps down, the server's impulse snaps it back one RTT later: a 50–150 cm double correction. Fix (B25): (i) `ShotSeqCounter` restarts per life — server in `ResetToLoadout` (next to `SwitchEndTime = 0`), owning client when the ASC avatar changes (`AVeloxCharacter::InitAbilityActorInfo`, autonomous proxy only), so the first shot of every life is 1 on both ends and the fresh CMCs' 0 is consistent; (ii) the server also advances `Confirmed` on every accepted shot without self-knockback potential while nothing is unresolved (`UVeloxMovementComponent::ServerNoteShot(Seq)` from the hitscan ability's accepted path), so the distance never exceeds the shots fired in one RTT. Tests: `KnockbackSeq` gains the scenario rows (fresh life, seq 1 vs 0 → replay; 200 hitscan shots then a rocket with and without (ii)); `Velox.Combat.Inventory` asserts the counter reset.
2. **Must-fix (06b) — test dummies die after two rails and respawn as the default pawn at a random start.** `GameModes/VeloxGameMode.cpp:68–89` (`NotifyControllerDied`, AI → `TryRespawn` → `RestartPlayer`), `Bots/VeloxDummySpawner.cpp:40–75` (spawns `DummyClass` at the spawner; nothing remembers it), `Bots/VeloxDummyController.cpp:21–31` (`StrafeOrigin` = wherever it was possessed). 08's `FireRun` fires 10 rails at a 200-hp dummy: it dies at shot 2, respawns 1.7 s later as `DefaultPawnClass` at a farthest-half start, strafing there; shots 3–10 miss or hit the wrong thing. Fix (B28): `AVeloxDummyController` keeps `RespawnTransform` and `RespawnPawnClass` (set by the spawner); `AVeloxGameMode::RestartPlayer` override uses `RestartPlayerAtTransform` and `GetDefaultPawnClassForController_Implementation` returns that class for such controllers; `OnPossess` restarts the strafe from `RespawnTransform`. `AVeloxDummySpawner::bInvulnerable` (default false; `SpawnDummy … invulnerable` argument) applies an infinite `GE_SpawnProtection` after possession; a protected hit still confirms (`FireHitscan.cpp:316–321` sends on `Hits.Num() > 0`, damage 0), so 08's hit rules hold. Test: `DeathFlow`-style world: kill the dummy → respawn within `MinRespawnDelay` at the spawner transform, class `DummyClass`, strafing.
3. **Should-fix (05b) — projectile server path bypasses 04b's validation and never finalises a pending switch.** `Weapons/VeloxGameplayAbility_FireProjectile.cpp:225–250` (`ConsumeServerFireToken` + hand-rolled checks; no `ValidateServerShot`, no `FinalisePendingSwitch`), `:271–296` (B15 copy). Consequences today: a NaN origin is snapped instead of rejected (hitscan rejects `origin`), `ViewServerTime` is unchecked (unused, harmless), and a shot of the pending RL inside the switch tolerance fires correctly but leaves `CurrentIndex` on the old weapon until the server's own switch timer ends (replicated weapon wrong for ≤ 0.25 s). Fix per ruling 2: `VeloxShotValidation::Validate` accepts `FGameplayAbilityTargetData_VeloxProjectileShot` (same base checks; `FVeloxValidatedShot` gains `FireMoveTimeStamp` + finite check, reason `time`), `VeloxShotValidation::ValidateOrigin(World, Shooter, Origin, MaxErrorCm, OutReason)` shared with `TraceShot`, and `FinalisePendingSwitch(*Definition)` before `LaunchServerShot`. 4. **Should-fix (05b) — rockets and plasma never confirm hits.** `Weapons/VeloxProjectile.cpp:369–486` (`ServerExplode` applies damage, sends no `ClientConfirmHit`); the hitscan path does (`FireHitscan.cpp:316–321`), 06's HUD marker reads it. ## 3. Soundness of the move-timed self-splash and the KnockbackRun watch item

- **Partition invariance.** Client: `GetClientMoveTimeStamp()` is `ClientData->CurrentTimeStamp`, which `ReplicateMoveToServer` advances before `SetMoveFor`, so during a live `PerformMovement` it is the move's end stamp; the ability fires in `PlayerTick` before the frame's move (B6), so `FireMoveTimeStamp` is the previous move's end. Server: `ServerMove_PerformMovement` sets `ServerData->CurrentClientTimeStamp = ClientTimeStamp` before `MoveAutonomous` (`CharacterMovementComponent.cpp` l.10029), and the fire RPC precedes the frame's move RPC on the wire, so `client_ts == last_ts` without loss (the `VeloxProjectileShot` line carries both; 08 asserts `|client_ts − last_ts| ≤ 1/60 + RTT`). The event fires at the end of the first move with `ts ≥ EventTime` on both ends; a lost move merges into the next one and would shift the server's event by one move (the A28 signature, ≈ 10 cm), which the implementation prevents the A29/A32 way: the move at whose end the prediction was queued is recorded as an event move (`bSavedQueuedPredictedKnockback`, `PostUpdate_Record`) and `ChooseOldMove` (`VeloxNetworkMoveData.cpp:197–238`) carries the newest unacked event move with the next RPC — the consuming move has `bForceNoCombine`, so that RPC goes out at once. Same structure as the pad entry/launch pair.
- **Channels.** Client: `AddKnockback(Predicted, Seq)` → `PendingPredictedKnockback` (autonomous proxy only, additive) → consumed first in the next move, captured in `SetMoveFor`, forbids combining, assigned in `PrepMoveFor` iff unresolved. Server: `GE_Damage` (bSelf, bRadius) inside `PerformMovement` → `AddKnockback(Server)` → chained `PendingKnockback` → consumed in the next move; the snapshot for the event move's check (`ServerMoveHandleClientError` runs after `MoveAutonomous`) carries `PendingKnockback = impulse` and `Confirmed = Seq`, so a correction for the event move replays with the chained value and drops the recorded one; a correction for an earlier move replays with the recorded one (`Confirmed = Seq − 1`); a later one has the impulse in `NewVel`. No double application in any of the three; verified by reading, consistent with `KnockbackSeq`.
- **Ordering server-side.** With `FwdSim = ping/2` the real projectile explodes ≈ ping/2 before the owner's move stream reaches `EventTime`: `ServerExplode` sees the pending event, matches `Origin` to `SelfSplashOrigin` within `ProjectileRadiusCm`, skips the owner and arms the deadline (`FwdSim + MaxRewindSeconds`); the event then runs in the move stream. A pawn hit or a different spot cancels the event and resolves the owner at the explosion (one correction, the ADR's original behaviour); the owner's client cancels its event from the impact cue when the cue location differs. Residual, accepted: at `FwdSim ≈ 0` the event can run first and a pawn stepping into the last centimetres of the flight gives the owner a phantom splash; probability negligible. Client-side `ComputeWorldFlight` and the server's use the same quantized origin/direction, the same channel and the same owner-only ignore list; Chaos sweep determinism across processes is assumed (bit-equal in the two runs) — a last-ulp difference would matter only if a move stamp lay within 1e-7 s of `EventTime`.
- **KnockbackRun watch item — hypothesis from the logs, no code change in 05 implicated.** Server log: `VeloxNetSoak event=knockback_server` at frame 750, then `VeloxMoveGap ts=1.808449 dt=0.033335` and `VeloxMoveRepaired ts=1.808449` at frame 753 — the move that consumed the launch was a merged 2/60 move (one client RPC lost right at the launch), so the server's grace at its end was already `0.2 − 0.0333 = 0.1667`, exactly the rule's limit. The first correction the client received was for the *next* move (`VeloxCorrectionResponse ts=1.825117 grace=0.149997`), i.e. `0.2 − 0.0333 − 0.0167 = 0.150` — the right value for that stamp; the echo chain continues it exactly (`0.150 → 0.0999 (Δts 0.050003) → 0.0167 (Δts 0.083339) → 0`), which is the A18 relation holding, not a container that lost the field. Why the correction for 1.808449 itself did not arrive (lost response at 2 % or not generated for the repaired move) is irrelevant to the field's integrity. 05's changes on this path are additive only (`ServerFillResponseData` copies one more field from the same snapshot, `Serialize` appends one `SerializeOptionalValue<uint8>` after the existing fields in the same order both ways, `ConsumePendingKnockback` keeps the M1 computation for the chained channel, `IsEventMove` adds a flag that is false in this script). Expected failure rate of the current rule ≈ 2–4 % per run (a lost RPC at one of the ~2 moves around the launch, plus a lost first response), independent of 05/06; the 06 report's earlier `grace 0.133` (= 0.2 − 4/60) is the same class. Fix is defect 9 (exact relation). If the dev's 10-round soak shows a failure *without* a `VeloxMoveGap` at the launch and with a first-response stamp equal to the launch move's, reopen this.

## 4. Checked and found correct (no change)

- **Projectile replication and roles.** `COND_InitialOnly` launch data, `DORM_DormantAll`, no movement replication, `NetCullDistance` 100 m, destruction replicates; the owner hides the real one and keeps the fake (`BeginPlay`), proxies dead-reckon in `GetServerWorldTimeSeconds()` and stop at the world; the impact cue is executed in `FScopedPredictionWindow(ASC, FPredictionKey(), false)` so the owner receives it even when the explosion happened inside the fire RPC's forward sim (B21 policy adopted from the start). `OnImpactCue` matches fakes by `(Owner, ShotSeq)` from the Velox effect context.
- **Explosion math.** Direct victim never the owner (`Step` ignores it), direction = flight direction; radius damage to every registered pawn but the direct victim with `Dist < R` and three-point LOS, zero points included (B3); `bSelf` from ASC identity → half damage, full knockback; world-only LOS on the `Weapon` channel. `ProjectileSim` covers advance, forward-sim stop, world flight, lifetime, pawn before/after wall, splash exclusion, LOS points, self half damage, protected → no knockback. `ProjectileDummy` covers the listen-host path (direct, splash, PG, rocket-jump apex); the remote path (fake, event, forward sim) is covered only by the online `RocketJumpTest` (two runs) — 08 makes it a soak (§5).
- **Recorded channel plumbing.** `SetMoveFor` captures before the move consumes; `CanCombineVeloxState` false when either side has one; `ClientUpdatePositionAfterServerUpdate` saves the live prediction, lets each replayed move take its recorded one, restores the live one — the queued-at-the-end-of-the-last-saved-move case is right. `FVeloxPredictedKnockback::Add` keeps the newer sequence (wrap-aware); two predictions in one move sharing the newer sequence is the documented residual and is harmless (both resolve by then).
- **Server bookkeeping.** `ServerBeginSelfKnockbackShot` moves `Confirmed` to `Seq − 1` only when nothing is unresolved (so an out-of-order resolution of two in-flight rockets never confirms the later one early), `ServerResolveSelfKnockbackShot` takes the newer; the deadline timer is bound weakly to the CMC; `RunMoveTimedEventNow`/`RunDueMoveTimedEvents` take callbacks out of the list before running them (a callback may kill the pawn).
- **Death flow steps 1–3** are inside the killing execution: `GE_Dead` first, then `CancelAbilities`, `ClearAbilityInput`, `RemoveAllGameplayCues`, `Inventory->StopBeam()` (B23), `UnregisterHitbox` (B17), `StartDeath`, `bIsCorpse`, death cue, `PlayerDied`; the `DeathFlow` test asserts the order from the log and that a second 1000-damage GE in the same frame yields nothing. Killer resolution from the instigator (PlayerState or pawn) handles telefrag and world kills; Q3 scoring (+1, −1 on suicide and world).
- **Respawn.** `ShouldSpawnAtStartSpot` false so every spawn goes through `ChoosePlayerStart`; occupied = living pawn inside the capsule volume; telefrag only when all are occupied, protection removed first, `bNoArmor`/`bNoKnockback`, `Velox.Damage.Telefrag`; `RestartPlayerAtPlayerStart` computes occupants before `Super` (the new pawn is nudged by `AdjustIfPossibleButAlwaysSpawn`, the occupant still dies — Q3). `ServerRequestRespawn` reliable, throttled by real time, accepted only after `MinRespawnDelay`; forced at `ForceRespawnDelay`; `Logout` clears timers. `ResetToLoadout` → `GE_SpawnInit` → `GE_SpawnProtection` on every possession, exactly once (the 04 hand-off was respected).
- **Clients.** `OnRep_PlayerState(null)` detaches the health component and clears the avatar only if still this pawn; `OnRep_IsCorpse` → `ApplyCorpseState` (collision off, body visible to the owner, dimmed colour); `CalcCamera` on the corpse with a camera-channel back-off; HUD overlay from `PawnLostTime` set in `SetPawn(nullptr)`.
- **Colour.** First unused palette index per PlayerState in `BeginPlay` (the PS is already in `PlayerArray`), `OnRep` → `ApplyPlayerColor`, palette test asserts ≥ 8 bright, saturated, distinct colours; `PlaceholderBody` ignores the `Weapon` channel and is owner-no-see until death.
- **Hit marker.** `ClientConfirmHit` state on the controller, 0.25 s white X, red and larger on a kill.
