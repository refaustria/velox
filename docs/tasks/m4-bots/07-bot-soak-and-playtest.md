## Report back
Standard dev format; per round: kills by bots, pickups by bots (total and per major item), link traversals and the longest link `dt=`, `VeloxBotStuck` with its `rule=` histogram and positions, `max_dwell_s` and where the longest dwell was, `VeloxBotRepath` count, off-mesh recoveries, refused count, hear/dodge counts, budget `mean_us` / `max_us` / `max_path_us` / `max_fire_us` / `paths=/s` / `max_deferred_frames`, the `goal=` histogram and the armed fight share, the all-unreachable evaluation counts by `start=`; the set totals (Σ strikes, Σ kills, major items covered); the final `FightBaseScore` if it was moved; the head the acceptance rounds ran on (with or without 04g). Flag for the lead: `max_fire_us` > 2000 µs in more than a quarter of the windows (D14 → Combat profiling task), any `refused` (D16 → `Need`/pickup-rule bug), any `giveup` (D17), `max_deferred_frames` > 30 (review §3 minor 8), any `VeloxBotStuck` with |x| < 150 and |y| < 150 (a pocket strike after 04g → architect).
