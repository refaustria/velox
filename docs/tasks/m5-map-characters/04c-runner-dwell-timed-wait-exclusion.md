## Goal
The D34 dwell scan in both runners (`nettest.sh` for BotRun/BotDuel, `ffa_summary.py` for the FFA set) excludes **timed-wait samples** — a bot standing at the spawn point of an unavailable item it has chosen as an Item goal (ADR 0005 §7 timing, D16, D39) — exactly as it already excludes `camp=1` samples, with the definition and the constants of ADR 0006 E2 (b)/(c). The 5 s cap is untouched. Each round prints `timed_waits`, `wait_samples`, `wait_max_s`, `wait_capped` and the three E2 FLAGs; `--reeval` of the stored D51 sets keeps every verdict and the three stored 6–7 s rounds of the dwell report fall to 2 s. `infra/ci/dwellwhy.py` becomes the triage tool for the next dwell question (task 06 on DM1). No engine run is needed for the acceptance; the lead runs one live BotDuel set afterwards.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Definition (E2 (b)), identical in both runners.** Episode of bot B: opened by a `VeloxBotRoute bot=B goal=Item item=X … available=0 respawn_in=R` line at t₀ (any phase); closed by the first of `VeloxPickupRespawn item=DA(X)`, the next `VeloxBotRoute bot=B goal=` line (which may open a new episode), or t₀ + min(R, `WAIT_MAX_S`) + `WAIT_SLACK_S`. A Live `VeloxBot` sample of B is a timed-wait sample iff an episode is open for B, the sample reads `goal=item item=DA(X)`, and (when X has a `VeloxPickupSpawn at=` position) its 2-D distance to that position is ≤ `WAIT_RADIUS`. Such a sample is handled like a `camp=1` sample: `dwell.pop(name)`; the next non-excluded sample starts a new run. `DA(X) = "DA_Item_" + X.replace(".", "_")`. Constants next to `DWELL_RADIUS`/`DWELL_MAX` in both files: `WAIT_RADIUS = 300.0`, `WAIT_MAX_S = 20.0`, `WAIT_SLACK_S = 1.0`, `WAIT_LEAD_FLAG_S = 16.0`, each with a one-line E2 comment. `DWELL_MAX` stays 5. Without any `VeloxPickupSpawn` line for X the positional test is skipped and the round prints `wait_pos=n/a` once.
- [ ] **Output (E2 (e)).** The per-round dwell report line (`. max_dwell_s=… [...]` in nettest; the run line in `ffa_summary.py`) gains `timed_waits=<episodes with ≥ 1 excluded sample> wait_samples=<n> wait_max_s=<x.x> [<bot>:<Item_Short>:R=<r.r>,lead=<l.l>] wait_capped=<k>` (`wait_max_s` = largest sample time − t₀ over excluded samples; `Item_Short` = the DA name without `DA_Item_`). Same keys in `stats.json` and the FFA `.json`. The nettest set line prints `timed_waits=[a,b,c] wait_max_s=[x,y,z]`; `ffa_set.py` prints `wait_max_s=<max over runs>`. FLAGs, printed as `FLAG …` lines, never a FAIL: (1) `FLAG no timed-wait sample although <n> Item routes had available=0 (log-format drift? ADR 0006 E2)` — suppressed when any sample of the round has `difficulty=DA_Bot_Easy`; (2) `FLAG timed wait beyond the gate <bot>:<item>:lead=<l> > 16 s (router gate ADR 0005 §7/D16)` for every episode with `lead > WAIT_LEAD_FLAG_S`; (3) `FLAG timed wait capped x<k>` when `wait_capped > 0` (an episode whose respawn line came later than t₀ + R + `WAIT_SLACK_S`).
