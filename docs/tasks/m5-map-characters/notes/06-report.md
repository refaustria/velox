## Findings for the lead / E3 (not layout defects; no `Source/Velox/Bots/` change was made)

1. **Fight stand-offs (dwell and `fight_stall`).** A bot with `goal=fight` whose target is on another level or out of sight stands up to 10 .. 22 s (A ledge foot, ledge/balcony corners). Dwell cap 5 s fails on DM1 for this reason in every set; the E2 exclusions (camp, timed wait) do not cover it. Needs a bot decision (re-path to a position with line of sight, or give up the fight after N s) or an E3 ruling on whether a fight stand is excluded.
2. **`progress` stuck rule fires while airborne.** All 3 strikes of ffa6 and 11 of the 26 baseline strikes have `ground=0` (drops, pad flights, falls): the 3 s window straddles the flight. Repeated-pad loops are a layout matter (fixed), the false positive is a bot matter.
3. **Maneuver started in the air is judged "missed".** A drop whose flight ends on a pad (or any landing that immediately re-launches) is reported `missed` although the bot did what the link asks (14 of 25 baseline misses in set 3). Layout avoids it by not offering drop links that land on a pad; a bot-side fix would be to treat a landing that triggers an impulse as ok/replaced.
4. **2-D arrival with stacked items.** A bot under an item 640 cm above it counts as arrived and stands (set 5, RG over the Quad). Fixed in layout (Quad moved); a 3-D arrival test would remove the class.
5. **Drop link success is 89 .. 98 % across sets on the final layout** (ffa5 97.9 %, ffa6 89.2 %, BotRun 97.9 %, BotDuel 97.1 %). The remaining misses are the E dock N drops (x = +-450, `Drop_DockE_N` 4/27, `DockW_N` 4/20 in ffa6), the A ledge drops (2+2 of 15) and the slab W/E drops (3+2). A candidate not yet tried: move the dock N link x from +-450 to +-350 (the door axis; the doors are x 200 .. 500 and the E dock's east jamb is at 500). It was not committed because it cannot be evaluated without another 55 minute set.
6. **Cost** is not judged anywhere (host contended). FFA `budget_mean_us` 378 .. 448 at load 1.7 .. 3.8 on DM1 versus the band 300 on `L_ItemTest` at an idle host; E3 needs an idle-host re-run for any cost band.
7. **E2 on DM1.** `timed_waits` 0 .. 8 per round, `wait_max_s` <= 8.1 (BotDuel set 4 round 3: 8.1; BotRun set 1 round 2: 8.1), `wait_capped` 0 in every set; three rounds in 18 sets carry the FLAG "no timed-wait sample although N Item routes had available=0" (each with `timed_waits` 0 in that round: Medium bots with `lead <= 0`); no `beyond the gate` FLAG. The measure works on DM1; the FLAG text suggests format drift but the cause is the preset (Medium, short lead).
8. **Engine noise:** `LogClass: Error: StructProperty FDataflowToolNodeSnapshot::Date ...` is printed on a fresh binary's first launch and counts as an `errors=` FAIL in one FFA run per set; not a Velox error.
9. Task 05's count line says `step=64`, `navlink=22`; current map: `step=63`, `navlink=16` (3 JUMP_PAD, 2 ROCKET_JUMP, 11 DROP).
