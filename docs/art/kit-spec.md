# Modular kit spec (ADR 0006 sections 2, 5, 8; task 14)

Theme: concrete-and-steel bunker, low-poly, flat-shaded ("stylised brutalist"; map "Praesidium"). The kit is the
visual layer of `L_DM1`; every piece is `NoCollision`, collision lives on the hidden `Clip_*` boxes of the layout.

## Reproduce

```
blender -b --factory-startup --python Tools/blender/kit.py -- --out ArtSource/Export --tex ArtSource/Textures --blend ArtSource/Kit/kit.blend
UnrealEditor-Cmd Velox.uproject -run=pythonscript -script=$PWD/Tools/editor/create_kit_materials.py -nullrhi -unattended -nopause -nosplash -stdout
UnrealEditor-Cmd Velox.uproject -run=pythonscript -script="$PWD/Tools/editor/import_kit.py --kit ArtSource/Export --verify" -nullrhi -unattended -nopause -nosplash -stdout
```

`kit.py` writes the FBX files, the PNG/HDR textures, `kit.blend` and `ArtSource/Export/kit_manifest.json` (pieces,
triangle counts, lightmap resolutions, material table). The manifest is the single source for the Unreal scripts.
The same `kit.py` run asserts the budgets (<= 600 tris per piece, <= 25 pieces, <= 8 materials, <= 6 textures at
<= 1024 px) and the palette.

## Conventions

- Centimetres, Z up, 1 Blender unit = 1 cm. Module 400 cm; the layout grid is 100 cm.
- **Pivot** = the local origin = the nominal module corner: minimum X, minimum Y at the floor level the piece stands
  on. Exceptions: `Floor_400` (top face at Z 0, slab hangs 20 cm below), `Ceiling_400` and `Light_Fixture` (underside at
  Z 0), `Ledge_Edge` and `Bridge_400` (platform/deck top at Z 0), `Wall_Door400` (spans Z 300..400 only; origin at the
  wall's floor corner). Trims and frames may reach below the nominal Y min by 5 cm (door pieces, `Ledge_Edge`).
- **Walls**: local +Y is the room side. A wall's clip box is the 20 cm slab Y 0..20; the generator rotates a piece 180
  degrees about Z (and shifts by 400 / 20) when the room lies on the other side. Trims stand 5 cm out on the +Y face,
  never more (ADR 0006 section 2 protrusion rule: <= 5 cm into walkable space). Ceilings are >= 320, so everything that
  hangs (`Ceiling_Beam` bottom at ceiling - 40, `Light_Fixture` at ceiling - 20) stays above 250 cm.
- **Ramp** `Ramp_15`: top rises 107.2 cm (15 degrees) over 400 cm of run; wedge, 400 wide. **Stairs** `Stairs_5x20`:
  5 steps, 20 cm risers, 80 cm treads, 400 run, 100 cm total, 400 wide (the layout's `stairs` record with
  `riser 20, steps 5, tread 80`).
- Hard edges only, every face a tri or quad (no n-gons), flat normals exported (`mesh_smooth_type = FACE`).
- **UV0**: world-space planar projection per face, 1 UV unit = 200 cm. Texel density 2.56 px/cm (512 px textures; the
  trim texture is 256 px, 1.28 px/cm). Every piece sits on a 200 cm lattice, so tiling is seamless across the module.
- **UV1** (lightmap) is generated by Unreal at import: `bGenerateLightmapUVs`, `MinLightmapResolution 64`,
  `SrcLightmapIndex 0`, `DstLightmapIndex 1`. Lightmap texel size: floor 400 / 128 = 3.1 cm, wall and bridge about 6 cm,
  trims and pillars 32 px.
- Materials: one slot per mesh, 8 instances of `M_Kit_Master` (parameters `BaseTex`, `Tint`, `Roughness`,
  `EmissiveStrength`). Palette (style guide): sRGB tint V <= 0.5 and S <= 0.25 on all but the emissive `Light`, checked
  in `kit.py` and again on the saved instances by `create_kit_materials.py`.

18 pieces, 648 triangles in total. Textures (`T_Kit_Concrete`, `T_Kit_ConcreteFloor`, `T_Kit_Steel`, `T_Kit_Plate`
at 512 px; `T_Kit_Trim` at 256 px) are generated procedurally (noise, seams, rivets, tread pattern) in `kit.py`.
`TC_Sky_DM1` is a 128 px `TextureCube` made from a generated 256 x 128 gradient HDR (cool zenith, pale horizon,
neutral grey ground).

Not in the kit (the generator scales or leaves them): partial floor tiles (scale `Floor_400` in X/Y, flat so the
distortion is invisible), corner pieces (a `Pillar` or two overlapping walls), windows.

## What the human may replace

Any mesh in `/Game/Environment/Kit/Meshes` can be swapped for hand-made art without touching the layout, as long as
the new mesh keeps the name, the pivot and the bounding box in the table above, one material slot, and the budgets
(<= 600 triangles, UV0 for the material, lightmap UVs generated on import with the resolution above). Textures and the
`MI_Kit_*` tints are free to change within the palette rule; the emissive `Light` instance is the only exemption.
Collision stays on the clip boxes, so a replacement mesh cannot change movement or bot navigation.
