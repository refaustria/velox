# Weapon and item model spec (ADR 0006 sections 4.6, 8; task 13)

Six weapons (one mesh each, used as world model on `weapon_r` and as view model) and 17 pickup meshes, low-poly and
flat-shaded, generated by `Tools/blender/weapons_items.py` (Blender 4.3.2 headless on the build VM, ADR 0006 E0.5).
They are placeholders in the art sense: readable silhouettes and colours, nothing a human modeller would call finished.

## Reproduce

```
blender -b --factory-startup --python Tools/blender/weapons_items.py -- --out ArtSource/Export --tex ArtSource/Textures --blend ArtSource/Weapons/weapons.blend
UnrealEditor-Cmd Velox.uproject -run=pythonscript -script="$PWD/Tools/editor/import_art.py --static ArtSource/Export --verify" -nullrhi -unattended -nopause -nosplash -stdout
```

The Blender run writes `SM_Weapon_*.fbx`, `SM_Item_*.fbx`, `weapons_manifest.json` (triangles, bounds, muzzle position: the single
source for the importer's `--verify`), `T_Items_Palette.png` and `weapons.blend`. The importer builds `M_Items_Palette` and
`M_Weapon_Flat`, imports the meshes to `/Game/Weapons/Meshes` and `/Game/Items/Meshes`, then runs `create_cosmetics_content.py`
(`WorldMesh = ViewMesh`, `ViewOffset`, `ViewScale 1`, rocket light) and `create_item_content.py` (`DA_Item_*::Mesh`).
`import_art.py --static --fixture` is the self-test of the import path (engine cylinder, socket added through Python, deleted again).
`python3 -I Tools/blender/weapons_items.py` runs the geometry and budget checks without Blender.

## Conventions

- Centimetres, Z up, modelled in Unreal axes (X forward, Y right); Blender +Y exports to Unreal -Y, so the script mirrors Y and
  reverses the faces (same as `kit.py`). The manifest bounds are asserted against the imported mesh (0.5 cm).
- **Weapons**: grip origin at (0, 0, 0), barrel along +X, body above the origin, handle 15 cm below. An empty named
  `SOCKET_muzzle`, parented to the mesh, sits at the barrel tip; the FBX importer turns it into the socket `muzzle`
  (`UVeloxWeaponCosmetics::MuzzleSocket`). Budget: <= 1 500 triangles, one material; the six use 100 to 190.
- **Items**: origin at the base centre, height <= 60 cm, <= 400 triangles (the largest has 148). `AVeloxPickup` centres the mesh
  on its bounds so everything spins about its middle.
- **UV**: UV0 only. Every face maps to one texel of `T_Items_Palette` (16 x 1, nearest, no mips, sRGB), so flat shading has flat colour
  and one material serves all meshes. No lightmap UV (dynamic objects), no collision (`NoCollision`; pickups use their own trigger).
- **Materials**: `M_Items_Palette` (emissive strength 0.45) and `M_Weapon_Flat` (0.3): lit, base colour = palette texel, the same colour
  times `EmissiveStrength` as emissive, roughness 1, specular 0, so pickups and the view model stay readable in baked-dark corners.

## Palette (index: colour)

0 steel dark, 1 steel mid, 2 steel light, 3 white, 4 red, 5 orange, 6 yellow, 7 green, 8 mint, 9 cyan, 10 blue, 11 purple,
12 pale yellow, 13 pale blue, 14 dark red, 15 brass. Accent colours follow the weapon / item colours of `create_item_content.py`.

## Weapons (silhouette, length rear..muzzle in cm, accent)

| Weapon | Silhouette | X range | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Machine Gun | short barrel, large drum magazine, stock | -20 .. 47 | pale yellow |
| Shotgun | two parallel barrels, pump forend, stock | -26 .. 58 | orange |
| Plasma Gun | coil rings round a core, sphere emitter | -10 .. 49.5 | purple, cyan |
| Rocket Launcher | fat tube, flared rear, front and rear bands, sight box | -12 .. 58 | red |
| Lightning Gun | open frame: two converging prongs round a glowing rod | -16 .. 46 | pale blue, cyan |
| Railgun | long thin rail, three ring coils, tail fins | -12 .. 74 | mint |

View offsets (camera space, cm; `FirstPersonScale 0.6` shrinks them toward the eye): `(28, 15, -15)` MG and SG, `(26, 15, -15)` PG,
`(17, 15, -15)` RL, `(29, 15, -15)` LG, `(14, 15, -15)` RG. Rule: visible lower right, muzzle at about 45 cm in front of the eye
(`x = 75 - muzzle_x`, never closer than 2 cm behind the eye plane for the rear of the mesh). The table is `VIEW_OFFSETS` in
`create_cosmetics_content.py`; nobody has seen it on a screen yet, tuning is human work (Mac playtest).

## Items

Health: white cube with a raised plus on every face (5 green 14 cm, 25 yellow 22 cm, 50 orange 30 cm); Mega: blue octagonal prism
38 cm with a white plus. Armor: Shard = green stretched bipyramid 23 cm; Yellow and Red Armor = heater-shield plate (36 and 44 cm tall)
with a raised inner plate. Ammo: one 22 x 16 x 11 box with a steel lid band, colour = the weapon's, plus a small emblem (bullets,
shells, rocket, cell sphere, lightning bipyramid, slugs). Powerups: dark base disc, a tall crystal and four spikes in the powerup's
colours (Quad cyan, Haste yellow, Regen red, Battle Suit green; the Quad's cyan reads as its "glow" through the emissive term).
Weapon pickups show the weapon mesh itself.

## What the human replaces later

- Everything above with hand-modelled meshes: keep the file names (`SM_Weapon_<Name>`, `SM_Item_<Type>_<Name>`), the pivot rules, the
  `muzzle` socket and the one-material rule, then re-run `import_art.py --static ... --verify` or import by hand.
- Offsets and the muzzle/rocket light numbers in `create_cosmetics_content.py` after seeing the view model in the Mac playtest.
- The `weapon_r` socket offset on `SK_Velox` (hand grip alignment of the third-person weapon) is task 11's human tuning.
- Item colours must keep the style-guide rule (saturated, distinct hues, health = white/green cross family, armor = yellow/red,
  Mega = blue, ammo = weapon colour).
