## What is right (and is now the rule)

1. **The stand-off rule is the right shape and sits where it should.** It is a *yield*, not a strike (D30's reaction reused: `FightSuppressedUntil`, immediate route evaluation; perception, overlay, aim and fire untouched), so it cannot inflate the stuck sums or make a bot roam; the decision simply stops seeing the enemy for 4 s. The sliding window on *positions* (every sample of the last 3 s within 150 cm of now) covers exactly the classes D30's grounded-speed rule misses — dodge hops in place, a reached or unreachable last-seen point, a target on another level — and a fight that moves 150 cm in 3 s never fires. The anchor-free window is the right choice (an anchor placed on the way in delayed the first probe's yield to 5.7 s). `bWatched` excludes forced goals, links and already-suppressed bots; a goal change resets the window (`SetGoal`). Evidence: DM1 FFA ×10 max dwell 10 → 5 s with 0 `progress` strikes and 3 `fight_stall` strikes (task 06 ffa6: 7 strikes, 10 s), 17 yields in 10 runs; BotDuel (Hard, the preset that fights longest) 0 yields in 6 rounds (06b) — so a genuine stand-up fight does not trip it; L_ItemTest 0 yields in 9 rounds. `supp1` 0–8 % (D36 report-only expectation < 5 %; run 8 of the FFA set is 8.0 % with 6 yields) — a number for E3's report-only list, not a defect (see Deferred 1).
2. **The progress rule's airborne fix is D18 and D27 kept, not weakened.** Three facts are now separated: a *hop* (airborne ≤ 2·Jv/|g| + 0.15 = 0.825 s; the window runs, the strike waits for the next ground tick — the D18 slab hopper still strikes, unit row), a *flight* (longer; the window pauses without the D27 cap and `StartDist` follows the bot, so the follower is measured from where it lands), and the *overlay pause* (D27, bounded by 4 s, unchanged). A flight is bounded by gravity, so the missing cap cannot be abused the way an unbounded overlay pause could (D27's reason). The unit rows cover the three cases (pad flight straddling the window → 0 strikes; hop oscillation → strikes, on the ground; fall → one strike, after landing). DM1 FFA ×10: `progress` strikes 3 (all `ground=0`) → 0.
3. **The two new link outcomes extend E0.3 (b)'s "not an attempt" list for the right reason**, and both runners, the README and the set-line text were updated together. The impulse bookkeeping is sound: the pad's own launch (first impulse of a `JumpPad` traversal, on the ground tick or the first airborne tick) and the rocket jump's own splash set `bLaunchImpulseSeen`; any later impulse away from End is `Relaunched`; a traversal that began on the ground and lands off the end surface is still `Missed` (unit rows for every branch, including the pad landing 500 cm off → `Missed`). DM1 FFA ×10: 18 `relaunched`, 0 `air_start`; the 8 remaining Drop misses are genuine landings off the end surface (dock N 3 + 1, slab W/E 1 + 2, A ledge 1; `links-by-record.txt`).
4. **3-D arrival with the nav agent height as the vertical tolerance is the correct constant** (floor levels ≥ 207 cm apart on every Velox map, items 0–80 cm above their floor; unit rows at 640, 320, 80 cm). It is applied at all three places arrival is decided (`EnsureMove`'s sticky arrival, `AlreadyAtGoal`, `OnMoveCompleted`), and a reached *projected* path end on another level is treated as a failed move (`FailedMoves`, roam after `FailedMovesToRoam`) — a stacked item now fails loudly (a dwell at an item goal, caught by the 5 s cap) instead of silently satisfying the bot. `IsAtGoalTarget()` is true for a goal without a point (a visible fight), so D12's pathless fight is untouched.
5. **Tunables are config** (`FightStandoffSeconds`, `FightStandoffRadiusCm`, `StuckProgressFlightSlackSeconds` in `[/Script/Velox.VeloxBotController]`, asserted by unit rows); the 0.25 s sample period is an implementation constant of the rule, documented in the header, acceptable like the 1 Hz debug line. No `Movement/` or lag-compensation file is touched; the maneuver core stays world-free (`FVeloxManeuverInput` gains `HeightAboveEndCm`, filled by the follower).
6. **Finding 5 was tried, measured and reverted with the number** (dock N at x = ±350: 8 of 10 missed against 5 of 5 before; the bots clip the door jamb). That is how a layout probe should end.
7. **Evidence discipline:** every set on a quiet host (load 0.07–1.63, no foreign UE process), D48 (4)(ii) recipe, `--reeval`-able, the L_ItemTest regression inside the D51 bands (BotRun 3/3 on the E0.3 (b) re-run, BotDuel 3/3, FFA ×3 PASS), build 119 tests, `fts.sh` 8/8.

## Defects (ranked)

**D1 — Medium — `AirStart` is asymmetric: a traversal begun in the air loses its *miss* but keeps its *success*.** `FVeloxBotManeuver::TickAirborne` finishes a landing away from End as `AirStart` (not an attempt) when `!bGroundSeen && !bLaunchImpulseSeen`, but the same traversal landing at End is `Ok` (counted). On a Quake-movement bot "begun in the air" is not the exception: a bunny-hopping bot is airborne 0.675 s of every hop, so a Drop segment that becomes current mid-hop is the ordinary case, and the implementer's rationale ("did not choose this flight") does not hold for a hop — the follower steered into the link while hopping. As written the clause biases the Drop rate upward whenever a hop-started drop misses. It fired 0 times in 06b's 20 rounds (`air_start=0` everywhere), so no committed number is wrong, but E3 judges exactly this invariant on every future set. *Fix (06c):* the follower records at `BeginManeuver` whether the pawn is in a **flight** in the controller's sense (airborne longer than `HopAirSeconds`: a pad launch, a drop, knockback) — `FVeloxManeuverInput::bBeganInFlight`, stored by `Begin`; `AirStart` is returned only when `bBeganInFlight` (a foreign flight), a hop-started traversal stays `Missed`/`Ok` like a ground start. The controller exposes its `IsFlight` result (`AVeloxBotController::IsInFlight()` from `State.AirborneSince`, or the follower tracks `AirborneSince` itself from `Movement->IsOnGround()` — the latter keeps the follower independent of the controller's tick order and is preferred). Unit rows: drop begun 0.3 s into the air (a hop), landing away → `Missed`; begun 1.0 s into the air (a flight), landing away → `AirStart`; both landing at End → `Ok`.

**D2 — Low — `Ok` on an external impulse "at the end level" has no horizontal bound.** `TickAirborne`: a non-own impulse finishes `Ok` when `DistToEnd <= EndAcceptanceCm` **or** (Drop) `HeightAboveEndCm <= EndAcceptanceCm / 2` — the second clause alone, so a drop kicked by a rocket (or a pad) at landing height 800 cm from End counts as a success and the follower continues the path from there. Dormant on the current layout (no DROP link's landing zone holds a pad since task 06 removed those links; the rocket-at-landing-height case needs a hit in a ≈ 50 cm band) — 0 observed — but it is the one clause that can turn a layout miss into an `ok`. *Fix (06c):* require `In.DistToEnd <= 2 × EndAcceptanceCm` as well (the end-level clause then means "landed next to the end and was kicked"), else `Relaunched`; unit row: impulse at end level 400 cm from End → `Relaunched`; at 150 cm → `Ok`. The follower's `IsOnEndSurface` is not pulled into the core (world-free).

**D3 — Low — the engine-noise ignore has never been exercised.** `ENGINE_NOISE_RE` is applied at the two sites that count errors in `nettest.sh` (bot rounds, `DeathSpectateRun`) and in `ffa_summary.py`; the message did not appear in 06b's runs. The regex is anchored on the exact message, as the task asked. *Fix (07b):* a fixture under `infra/ci/fixtures/noise/` (a BotRun round log with the line injected) that `--reeval` must print with `errors=0`; and the same line injected into one `ffa-*.log` copy for `ffa.sh --reeval`.

**D4 — Low — `result=not_at_goal` is invisible to the soak.** The 3-D arrival turns a stacked-item goal into `VeloxBotPath … result=not_at_goal failures=n` lines and, after three, a goal drop; neither runner counts the line (grep: no `VeloxBotPath` parse), so a DM1 layout that stacks an item over walkable floor would show up only as a dwell. *Fix (07b):* count `not_at_goal` per round next to `no_clear_point` (report-only, `not_at_goal=<n>` on the detail line and in `stats.json` / the FFA `.json`; a set-line sum). E3 lists it as report-only with the expectation 0 on DM1.

## Deferred, not defects

1. **Repeated yields are not escalated.** `FightYields` is counted and logged, never read: a bot that yields, routes 4 s, returns to the same ledge corner and yields again can do so for a whole round without a strike (D30's `fight_stall` escalates to roam on the third strike; the yield does not). Run 8 of the DM1 FFA set shows 6 yields / 8.0 % `supp1` from one such cycle. Not changed now: the yield is the correct first response and the number is small; E3 keeps `fight_yields` and `supp1` report-only with the D36 expectation (`supp1` < 5 %) and the rule that a confirmation set with `supp1` > 5 % in two or more runs makes this a `dev-hard` task (escalate the k-th yield within `3 × FightStallCooldownSeconds` of the previous one to a `fight_stall` strike).
2. **The L_ItemTest 9 s dwell (it-botrun round 2) is a known class, not a 06b regression, and gets no task now.** Trail (server log 04:27:56–04:28:09): Celer (Medium) respawns, routes to the YA (`travel 3.37`, `available=1`), reaches the ramp foot in 4 s and is then under fire from `VeloxCharacter_8` for 8 s (`vis=1`, `fire=held|fire`, speed 31–433, `toward=−0.44`), dodging around a path point 16 cm away that never advances (the engine's intermediate-point test is "passed along the segment direction", and the overlay-owned dodge never carries it past); the D27 progress rule pauses 4 s and strikes at 6.0 s as designed (`rule=progress dist=16 window_dist=16 ground=1`); one second later the enemy dies (`VeloxCharacter_8` → `_9`), the overlay releases, the bot walks 2.5 m and takes the YA (04:28:09.239). None of 06b's rules applies (Item goal, grounded, not at the goal, no link). The class is an **item-goal fight pin**: the D23 blend under fire fails to move a bot whose next path point is a corner a few cm ahead. Frequency: 1 in 06b's 28 rounds and runs (15 bot rounds on DM1 and L_ItemTest, 13 FFA runs), and E2's re-scan of 41 stored Duel rounds found no run of this class (its two stalls above 5 s were `goal=roam` stalls) — rare; E0.3 (b)'s re-run-once rule handled it (3/3 on the re-run). *Trigger for a task:* a second occurrence in the E3 confirmation sets or the task 15 soaks (≥ 2 in ≤ 30 rounds) makes it `dev-hard`: on a D27 strike under an Item goal with a visible enemy, switch the goal to Fight for the cooldown (so D30 and the stand-off apply and the bot commits to the fight it is in) rather than tuning `FightPathWeight`. Recorded in E3.
